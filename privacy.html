How Kronos collects, uses, and shares personal information — and the choices you have.

This Privacy Policy explains how Kronos Fusion Energy, Inc. ("Kronos," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit this website or interact with us, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Cookie Policy.

01Information we collect

Information you provide. When you contact us, request a briefing, submit a partnership or investor inquiry, apply for a role, or subscribe to updates, we may collect your name, email, organization, role, and the contents of your message or application.

Information collected automatically. When you use the site, we may collect log and device data (IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring pages, and timestamps) and, where you consent, analytics data via cookies and similar technologies.

02How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide requested materials.
  • To evaluate partnership, investor, and employment inquiries.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the site and our communications.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

03Legal bases (where GDPR applies)

We process personal information on the bases of your consent, our legitimate interests (such as operating and improving the site and responding to inquiries), the performance of a contract or steps taken at your request, and compliance with legal obligations.

04Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy. Where required, non-essential cookies (including analytics) are set only with your consent.

05How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with: service providers and processors who act on our behalf (for example, hosting, email, and analytics); professional advisors (legal, accounting); authorities where required by law or to protect rights and safety; and, in connection with a merger, financing, or other business transaction, with appropriate safeguards.

06International transfers

Kronos is based in the United States, and your information may be processed in the U.S. and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

07Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

08Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; and to withdraw consent. Residents of California and similar jurisdictions may request access or deletion and may opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — Kronos does not sell personal information. To exercise any right, contact us via our contact page. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

10Children

This site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 (or under 13 where applicable).

11Third-party links

The site may link to third-party websites and services with their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for their content or policies.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the "last updated" date reflects the current version. Material changes will be indicated on this page.

13Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Kronos Fusion Energy, Inc. via our contact page.
