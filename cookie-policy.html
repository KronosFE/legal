Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

How Kronos uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

This Cookie Policy explains how Kronos Fusion Energy, Inc. ("Kronos") uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and tags. They allow a site to recognize your device, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used.

02How we use them

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site to function (for example, security and basic navigation). These are always active.
  • Preferences — remember choices such as display or language settings.
  • Analytics — help us understand aggregate, de-identified usage so we can improve the site. Where required by law, these are set only with your consent.

We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

03Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services we use (for example, analytics or embedded media such as videos and our live simulation). These providers may process data in accordance with their own policies.

04Managing cookies

Where required, we present a cookie banner allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect site functionality. For visitors in applicable jurisdictions, you may exercise rights described in our Privacy Policy, including choices regarding analytics and any "Do Not Sell or Share" preference.

05Changes and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time; the "last updated" date reflects the current version. Questions may be directed to Kronos via our contact page.
